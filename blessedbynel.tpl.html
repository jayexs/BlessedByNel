Booking Policies

Deposit

A $20 non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment.

Late fee

A few minutes late is fine. 25–30 minutes late is a $10 fee. Please let me know if you're running late.

Hair

I don't provide hair, so please bring your own. Having trouble finding it? Let me know.

Guests

I prefer no guests. If you need to bring someone, please let me know in advance.
